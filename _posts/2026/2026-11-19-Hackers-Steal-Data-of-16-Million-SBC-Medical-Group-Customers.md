## Hackers Steal Data of 1.6 Million SBC Medical Group Customers 🚨

SBC Medical Group has issued a warning regarding two significant cybersecurity incidents that have compromised sensitive information of both customers and employees, affecting **1.6 million individuals**. This clinic management company, primarily operating in Japan with a limited presence in the U.S., is publicly traded on Nasdaq as SBC.

### Details of the Breach 🔍
The first incident involved unauthorized access to SBC databases, while the second incident pertained to unauthorized access to clinic administrator accounts on **Tribeau**, a third-party booking platform. The company discovered the database breach on **November 16, 2026**, and was notified of the Tribeau compromise on the same day. Both incidents primarily impacted its operations in Japan.

According to a FORM 8-K filing with the SEC, "On November 16, 2026, unauthorized access occurred to certain databases within a website system managed and operated by SBC Medical Group, Ltd., the Company's Japanese subsidiary, in connection with its management services in Japan." The breach exposed basic personal information of **1,624,000 people**, including:
- Full names
- Contact details
- Dates of birth
- Patient card numbers
- Member IDs
- Passwords
- Survey responses

Not all data types were exposed for every individual. Additionally, the breach affected:
- **255,416** reservation requests
- **217,769** inquiries submitted to SBC websites
- Thousands of treatment description records, image filenames, and customer reviews.

### Employee Data Compromised 👥
The attackers also accessed approximately **20,000 employee account records**, which included names, IDs, and passwords. For a small subset of **459 employees**, the compromised data also included bank account and tax information.

The second incident involved administrator accounts on the Tribeau booking platform, which is utilized by **56 clinics**. The operator reported access to reservation information for **12,641** individuals, along with treatment names, dates, and fees for **6,804** patients.

### Ongoing Investigation 🔄
As of **November 18**, when the Form 8-K was submitted, SBC Medical Group has not observed any evidence of misuse of the exposed data. The investigation is ongoing, and the company has not yet estimated the financial impact of these cybersecurity incidents.
