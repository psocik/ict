## Cyberattack Disrupts Delhaize Supermarkets in Belgium

A **cyberattack** has impacted the **freezers** and **refrigerators** at dozens of **Delhaize** supermarket locations across Belgium. Reports indicate that at least **40 supermarkets** were affected by this incident, which the company described as an "IT incident" impacting their refrigeration systems. 🚨

The attack appears to have targeted multiple cooling systems linked to Delhaize's operations, occurring either late Sunday night or early Monday morning. As a result, the temperatures in these systems rose sharply, causing significant concern. ❄️

In addition to Delhaize, **Carrefour Belgium** has reported that **fifteen of their stores** have also been affected. Furthermore, Swedish media has highlighted a similar situation involving the **ICA supermarket chain**, where temperatures in their cooling systems reportedly soared to **50 degrees Celsius** overnight. This incident echoes a large-scale cyberattack on refrigeration systems in French supermarkets last month, which disrupted hundreds of retail locations. 🥵

Delhaize is owned by **Ahold Delhaize**, the same parent company as **Albert Heijn**. A previous cyberattack last month forced Ahold to shut down a wide range of its IT systems, impacting supply chain, distribution, and retail operations across various brands, including **Etos** and **Gall & Gall**. This led to empty store shelves and halted logistics, with the situation only returning to normal last week. 📉
