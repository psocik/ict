## Major Data Breach Exposes Medical Information 🚨

The New York attorney general (AG) has reached a settlement over a significant medical data breach affecting **13.3 million people** due to basic security failures. Perry Johnson & Associates (PJ&A) and four related companies have agreed to pay **$300,000** and implement extensive security reforms following this breach that exposed personal and medical information.

### Key Details:
- **Settlement Amount:** $300,000
- **Potential Penalties:** $9.7 million if terms are not met
- **Affected Individuals:** Over 13.3 million, including approximately 4.2 million New Yorkers

The breach was announced by New York AG Letitia James on **November 6**, alongside the attorneys general of Connecticut, Michigan, and New Jersey. The investigation revealed a series of failures, including:
- Remote access without multifactor authentication
- Nearly identical passwords circulated via email
- Sensitive files lacking file-level encryption
- Security software that detected malicious activity but failed to alert security staff

The companies only discovered the attack when the attackers contacted them on **May 2, 2023**, demanding payment. Notifications to affected individuals were also delayed, with PJ&A starting to contact clients in **July 2023**.

### Exposed Information:
The stolen database files contained:
- Names, dates of birth, and home addresses
- Social Security numbers
- Medical record and hospital account numbers
- Insurance information
- Admission diagnoses and treatment dates
- Clinical information from medical transcriptions, including test results and medications

This exposed information could be valuable to criminals, with our research finding stolen identity packages selling for as little as **95 cents** on the dark web.

### Required Security Changes:
The settlement mandates extensive security changes for the involved companies, including:
- Multifactor authentication for user accounts
- Stronger password management
- Encryption of personal and protected health information
- Monitoring that alerts security personnel to suspicious activity
- Annual risk assessments and penetration testing
- Network segmentation and measures to prevent unauthorized data transfers
- Written incident response procedures and clear rules for retaining sensitive information
