## Beware of Fake Security Scans! 🚨

A wave of deceptive websites is posing as Microsoft security scans, misleading victims into uninstalling their antivirus software. These sites, branded as SysScan, falsely claim that your computer has serious issues due to third-party antivirus programs. **This is a scam!** 🚫

### How the Scam Works:
- **Fake Security Scan:** The sites run a convincing but fraudulent scan, claiming your antivirus is the problem.
- **Data Collection:** They collect personal information under the guise of a refund process.
- **Remote Access:** Victims are often asked to install remote-access software, putting their security at risk.

### Warning Signs of This Scam:
- Claims of deep problems with your computer.
- Every result is negative.
- Requests to uninstall your antivirus.
- Demands for banking or cryptocurrency information.

If you encounter such a site:
1. Disconnect from the internet.
2. Reinstall your antivirus software.
3. Report the scam to the FTC and IC3.

Stay safe online! 🛡️
