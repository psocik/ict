## The Fake Sites Using a Cheap Toolkit to Sell $2,000 AI Subscriptions

🚨 We found more than 100 subscription websites linked through the same toolkit and closely related developer details. These sites often impersonate existing products, including **GPT-6 Astra**, **DaVinci Resolve**, **PixAI**, and **OpenCut**. Moreover, another site uses the name of **Omegle**, the chat service that shut down in 2023. Additionally, others promote unfamiliar brands with little verifiable information about who operates them.

The sites we examined did not use fake password forms or push malware downloads. Instead, they consistently used polished product pages and genuine Google sign-in screens before directing visitors to paid plans. As a consequence, someone visiting an imitation site could easily believe they were buying from the genuine provider. Regarding the unfamiliar brands, it is particularly difficult to establish who is selling the subscription or independently verify the claims being made. Importantly, despite their different names and designs, the sites share identical underlying files and closely related developer email addresses. This consistency strongly suggests that a single operator, or closely connected group, is behind the entire network.

At first glance, these sites don't look suspicious. They notably employ secure connections, professional designs, and the confident language you would expect from an established software company. However, it is vital to remember that the secure connection and padlock only show that traffic between you and the site is encrypted. They do not confirm who operates it. Furthermore, some sites show performance comparisons, star ratings, and precise user numbers to enhance their apparent legitimacy.
