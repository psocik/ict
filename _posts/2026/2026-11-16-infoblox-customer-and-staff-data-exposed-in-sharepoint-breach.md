## Infoblox Customer and Staff Data Exposed in Sharepoint Breach 🚨

Networking and security firm **Infoblox** is currently investigating a significant third-party breach that allowed unauthorized individuals to access sensitive customer and staff data. A company spokesperson confirmed to *The Register* that, "Infoblox is investigating a security event involving unauthorized access to certain company information through a compromised third-party data migration vendor."

According to sources, this vendor had trusted access to Infoblox's SharePoint system, and the intruders exploited this access to exfiltrate data directly from Microsoft SharePoint. The compromised data included configuration information related to some customers' **NIOS (Network Identity Operating System)** deployments. These snapshots of customers' network data, such as DNS, DHCP configurations, Grid databases, and reporting logs, are crucial for recovering the appliance or grid in case of a system failure or faulty upgrade.

The breach also resulted in the theft of employee data. An email from CEO **Scott Harrell** to employees, which *The Register* has seen, revealed that compromised employee details include financial information such as direct deposit details, contact information, dates of birth, and Social Security numbers for employees and their dependents.

Upon discovering the breach, Infoblox "immediately activated" its incident response procedures and took steps to contain the incident, including hiring outside cybersecurity experts. The spokesperson stated, "We have sent notifications about this security event and are providing support and guidance as appropriate. Based on our investigation to date, we have no indication that any Infoblox products, cloud services, software updates, or build-and-release processes were involved. Our priority is to fully investigate and resolve this issue."

Affected staff received a **$100 "spot bonus"** to purchase password management services and were warned to be especially alert for phishing, vishing, fraud, or other suspicious communications. Infoblox also provided employees and their dependents with **free credit monitoring and identity protection** for two years.
