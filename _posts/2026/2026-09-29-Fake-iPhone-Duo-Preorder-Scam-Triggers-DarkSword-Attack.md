## Fake iPhone Duo Preorder Scam Triggers DarkSword Attack 🚨

A new scam has emerged that mimics a preorder page for the iPhone Duo, designed to exploit vulnerabilities in iPhones using the leaked DarkSword exploit chain. This deceptive page, which boasts an Apple-like design and a tempting **$500 voucher**, aims to infiltrate your device without any interaction required from you. Just opening the page can initiate the attack! 

### What You Need to Know:
- The scam page resembles Apple's official site, complete with its logo and a footer claiming "Copyright © 2026 Apple Inc."
- Apple has not yet opened preorders for the iPhone Duo, which are set to begin on **October 16**. Therefore, any preorder attempts now are fraudulent.
- The exploit checks your iOS version and, if successful, gains deep access to your device, potentially stealing sensitive information such as saved credentials, cryptocurrency wallet data, and notes.

### Potential Risks:
If the exploit is successful, the malicious payload can:
- Contact its server with device identifiers and installed app lists.
- Access and upload files from your device, including wallet files and personal data.
- Execute commands from the server to gather more information or manipulate your device.

### Stay Safe:
To protect yourself from such scams:
- Always keep your iPhone updated to the latest software.
- Avoid clicking on unfamiliar links, especially those claiming to be preorder pages.
- If you suspect you have visited a malicious site, restart your iPhone after updating.
