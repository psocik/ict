## Warning: Phishing Alert! 🚨

Call of Duty Mobile players should be vigilant against a **phishing campaign** disguised as a free Call of Duty Points giveaway. Victims are prompted to log in with their email address and password to claim free Call of Duty Points (CP), the game's premium currency. After this, they are redirected to a second page requesting their two-factor authentication (2FA) code. 

It's crucial to note that this site has no connection to Activision. Its sole purpose is to **steal login details** needed to take over accounts.

### Why This Matters
Call of Duty Mobile has been downloaded approximately **489 million times** worldwide and has generated around **$1.8 billion** in lifetime in-app purchases. An Activision account can hold more than just in-game currency; many players link their accounts to Xbox, PlayStation, or Battle.net. A stolen login could expose:
- Stored payment methods
- Purchase history
- Other linked gaming accounts

### How the Scam Works
The initial page mimics the official Call of Duty Mobile site and offers **10,800 free points** in exchange for an email address and password—not a redemption code, but a full account login. This scam claims the reward will be "confirmed" within **four to eight hours**, buying time before anyone notices nothing has arrived. 

**Warning Signs to Look For:**
- The page says "GET FREE POINT" instead of "GET FREE POINTS."
- Awkwardly worded instructions.
- A live chat widget that seems to exist solely to make the site appear more legitimate.

Stay safe and protect your accounts! 🔒
