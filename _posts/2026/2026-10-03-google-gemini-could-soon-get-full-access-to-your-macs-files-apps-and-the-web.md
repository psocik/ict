## Google Gemini Could Soon Get Full Access to Your Mac's Files, Apps and the Web

🚀 **Exciting news for Mac users!** Google's Gemini could soon have the ability to access any file on your macOS device, open apps, browse the web, and perform actions without needing your permission every time. As spotted by TestingCatalog on X, Google is currently testing desktop control for Gemini, with references to a new hidden "Additional sandbox options" setting in the Gemini Desktop app.

🔍 Although this feature isn't live yet and Google hasn't confirmed it, when enabled, Gemini could read, create, modify, or even delete files anywhere on your PC. This means that Gemini will have access to files outside the folders you have explicitly connected to it.

📧 According to a pop-up within Gemini Desktop, Google's AI could also communicate with apps such as Mail, Safari, or Messages and perform actions through them. Google explains that by enabling additional sandbox options, you will be able to expand what Gemini can do and access on your Mac. Depending on which settings you enable, Gemini may be permitted to take actions without asking for your permission first.

🔒 However, Google is not giving Gemini unlimited control without safeguards. You can expect a Claude-like experience, where you explicitly give it permission to use your PC. Gemini would still ask for confirmation before buying products, transferring money, creating online accounts, accepting legal terms on your behalf, or modifying sensitive information about you.

🖥️ This setting appears to be part of Google's broader plans for Gemini, where the AI would be able to work across files, websites, and native apps instead of being limited to a chat window. It's still unclear how this feature will play out, especially as Apple is considering making it difficult for AI agents to access personal files and data on Mac.

🔜 It's uncertain when Google plans to roll out Full Access or which Gemini model will power it.
