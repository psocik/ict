## Who Runs the Ransomware Group Qilin?

The cybercrime group **Qilin** has emerged as the world's most active ransomware affiliate program, claiming at least **365 victim organizations** in the past three months alone. The administrator of Qilin has made a few key missteps over the last decade that all point to a **36-year-old man** living in **St. Petersburg, Russia**. Operating since **September 2022**, the Russia-based ransomware group Qilin is currently the most industrious ransomware-as-a-service gang, according to a report this month by the cybersecurity firm **NCC Group**. Qilin attracts affiliates by offering them at least **80 percent** of any extortion amount paid by victims. Its members practice the **"double extortion"** model, stealing sensitive data before encrypting systems and threatening to publish the files online. Qilin is happy to team with English-speaking affiliates, and its leader has shared on **Telegram** that they've worked with cybercrime groups like **Scattered Spider** and **RansomHub**.

The head of Qilin goes by the handle **"Haise."** The cyber intelligence platform **Intel 471** records that in **June 2022**, Haise applied for membership to **RAMP**, a Russian cybercrime forum. These messages show the RAMP administrator approved Haise's application, referring to this user as Haise and by their Telegram username: **Alexbigboss44**. Searching Intel 471 for Alexbigboss44 reveals this was the Telegram account name shared by the user **Rjun** on the Russian cybercrime forums **Exploit** and **XSS** two years earlier in **2020**. The cyber intelligence platform **Flashpoint** finds Alexbigboss44 is a prolific participant in cybercriminal forums and Telegram channels, primarily focused on the illicit trade of traffic, logs, and corporate access. Flashpoint says this user was engaged in high-volume acquisition of **Facebook logs** and **Google Ads accounts** to facilitate fraudulent advertising and gambling schemes, and that they functioned as a broker and operator.

Intel 471 finds that by the time Alexbigboss44 was applying for membership to RAMP, Rjun was already a longtime member of several other Russian crime forums. Intel 471 says Rjun signed up at all of these sites with the email address **mail-30a@mail.ru**. The identity threat protection firm **Spycloud** found the email address mail-30a@mail.ru at one time used the password **"Rjun132."** A search for that email at the breach tracking service **Constella Intelligence** shows that it appeared in records maintained by **gosuslugi.ru**, the official digital platform for state and municipal services in the Russian Federation. Gosuslugi was breached in late **2025**, reportedly exposing more than **20 million records** with full names, birth dates, and mobile numbers. According to the leaked Gosuslugi records, the address mail-30a@mail.ru belongs to **36-year-old Aleksey Mikhailovich Yakovtsev** from St. Petersburg, Russia. Flashpoint finds Haise has long used a Telegram account with the user ID **118329521**, recording more than **5,000 messages** since **2018**. A search on this Telegram ID in Constella shows it is linked to the Russian phone number **+79602347275**.
