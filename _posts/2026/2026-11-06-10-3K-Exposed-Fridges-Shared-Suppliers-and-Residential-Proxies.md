## 10.3K Exposed Fridges: Shared Suppliers and Residential Proxies

Infrawatch has identified a significant exposure, with **10.3K active refrigeration hosts** found across **127 countries and territories**. Their research highlights that recorded supplier relationships connect businesses identified in this investigation to retail networks in the **UK**, **France**, and **USA**. A critical finding is that **56 active residential proxy hosts** across **21 countries** also expose refrigeration interfaces. Infrawatch states that a supplier's refrigeration failure can spoil stock and interrupt deliveries, even while the customer's own systems keep running. This unified infrastructure intelligence connects exposed refrigeration, identified residential proxies, and recorded supplier relationships in one investigation. 🚀

Compounding these exposures, on **5 November 2026**, **CISA**, the **FBI**, and three other US agencies issued a joint warning. This alert addressed malicious activity specifically targeting refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems. They urged operators to take immediate action, including removing public exposure, isolating OT networks, and securing remote access. The Infrawatch report independently identified the **56 active residential proxy hosts** across **21 countries** that expose refrigeration interfaces. These same proxy hosts also expose other sensitive systems, such as power monitoring, energy management, building controls, databases, and remote desktops. ⚠️

Infrawatch notes that refrigeration risk extends beyond an organization's own network. They identified exposed refrigeration interfaces on networks associated with producers, logistics businesses, and laboratory manufacturing. Recorded supplier relationships connect that infrastructure research to retail supply chains. The US mapping includes pharmaceutical, healthcare, beverage, and produce suppliers with recorded relationships into **Publix**, **Walmart**, **Kroger**, **Costco**, and **Target**. A cooling failure at a shared supplier can interrupt deliveries to several customers. Claroty's **XWEB Pro** research describes a supervisor coordinating field controllers that operate compressors, cooling fans, and defrost heaters. One exposed management system can therefore affect several cabinets, cold rooms, or production processes. ❄️
