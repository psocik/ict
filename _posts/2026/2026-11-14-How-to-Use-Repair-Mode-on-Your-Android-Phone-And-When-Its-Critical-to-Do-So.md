## How to Use Repair Mode on Your Android Phone - And When It's Critical to Do So

Need to send your Android phone to a third-party service for repair? **Put it in Repair Mode first** so that no one handling your phone can access your installed apps or data. Android includes a special mode specifically for repairs, available for Pixel and Samsung phones. Your phone must be running at least Android 14. 🚀  

Repair Mode (or Maintenance Mode on Samsung devices) sets up a sandboxed, temporary profile on your phone that protects your personal information by locking down your data, while still allowing a technician to use all of the phone's functionality. **Consider Repair Mode a must-use feature** if you ever have to hand your phone over for repair. A repair technician cannot switch back to normal mode and access your data without your PIN or password. 🔒  

Before you do this, I recommend **backing up your data** just to be safe. How you switch to Repair Mode depends on whether you have a Pixel or a Samsung phone. Here are the steps for each:  
- **Pixel:** Go to Settings > System > Repair Mode and tap Enter repair mode. You'll be prompted to enter your PIN or password.  
- **Samsung:** To enable Samsung's Maintenance Mode, go to Settings > Device care > Maintenance Mode.  

Once you receive your phone back, disable Repair or Maintenance Mode by pulling down the Notification Shade and tapping Exit. You will be prompted for your PIN or password before the phone can switch back to normal mode. Use Repair Mode whenever you have to hand your phone in for repair, so you don't have to worry about your data being viewed or stolen. 📱
