## Norwegian Gas Giant Equinor Under Cyberattack 🚨

Norwegian Gas Giant Equinor has confirmed a cyberattack on its IT systems, but assures that production remains unaffected. On November 17, the company detected an ongoing cyberattack against some of its IT systems. Equinor, Europe's largest gas supplier, stated that it has mobilized relevant resources to neutralize the attack and regain full integrity.

> "Equinor has detected an ongoing cyber-attack against certain IT systems. Relevant resources have been mobilized to neutralize the attack and regain full integrity," reads the statement published by the company. 

The company further elaborated that since this is not a virus attack, there is no risk of the attack spreading. Currently, there is no indication that the attack has consequences for employees, partners, or suppliers. Production remains unaffected, and there is no evidence of data theft.

The disruption has impacted everyday work, affecting IT applications and internal systems, but not the wells. According to Norwegian news agency NTB, internal systems were affected, and a person at Equinor informed Reuters that the internal email system was down. Norway's National Security Authority (NSM) confirmed it was aware of the attack and is ready to provide support if needed.

This incident follows a recent increase in cyber threats across Norway. Last month, the NSM agency raised its cyber threat level to four on a five-level scale, warning that cyberattacks could disrupt critical services such as water and electricity. NSM also reported that successful cyberattacks against Norwegian companies had tripled in the previous six months. 

> "The incident currently being experienced by Equinor confirms that this risk is indeed high," an NSM spokesperson said on Tuesday.

This isn't Norway's first brush with this kind of pressure this year. In August, a pro-Russian group claimed a cyberattack that disrupted multiple Norwegian government digital services for several days. However, separate claims from that period do not establish who is behind this week's attack on Equinor, and nobody has named a group yet.
