## Pentagon Data Breach Impacts Millions of US Military Personnel

The US government is notifying nearly three million current and former military staff that their personal information was stolen during a breach of the Pentagon's personnel records. The stolen information included **Social Security numbers**, **names**, **dates of birth**, **contact information**, **demographic details**, and information about military jobs. 

The data breach notification from the Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) stated that attackers exploited a vulnerability in an unnamed file-sharing system and had access to the database from **October 2025** to **July 2026**. The breach affected **2.8 million** living people and around **300,000** who are deceased. The US military currently has approximately **1.3 million** active personnel. 🚨
