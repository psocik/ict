## 🚨 Data Breach Alert: Heights Finance Hack 🚨

A significant breach at Heights Finance has exposed personal and financial data of over **1.2 million** individuals after hackers compromised a third-party cloud platform. Heights Finance, a U.S. consumer finance company, primarily serves customers with limited access to traditional bank credit.

On **May 7, 2026**, Heights Finance discovered unauthorized access to a cloud platform storing sensitive customer data. The company has since launched an investigation with external cybersecurity experts and notified federal law enforcement.

### Key Details:
- **Unauthorized Access:** The breach was limited to the cloud-based platform and did not affect loan management systems or other networks.
- **Data Compromised:** The stolen information includes contact details, financial and bank account data, government IDs, and dates of birth.
- **Affected Individuals:** This may involve Heights Finance customers, loan applicants, and former borrowers of related brands.

### What’s Next?
Heights Finance is offering affected individuals **24 months of free credit monitoring** and identity protection. Fortunately, dark web monitoring has found no evidence that the stolen data has been published. As of now, no threat actor has claimed responsibility for the breach.
