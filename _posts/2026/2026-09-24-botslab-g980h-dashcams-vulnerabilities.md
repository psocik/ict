## Botslab G980H Dashcams Vulnerabilities 🚨

The **Botslab G980H Dashcams** have been found to have several critical vulnerabilities that could be exploited by attackers. Successful exploitation may allow unauthorized access to sensitive data, modification of device configurations, and disruption of device operations.

### Affected Versions:
- G980H dash cam series 30010_QHG980HN5294SysFW+
- G980H dash cam series 58_QHG980HMCN5291SysFW+

### Vulnerabilities Identified:
1. **CVE-2026-84399**: Authorization vulnerability in session-based command functionality.
2. **CVE-2026-88761**: Predictable WiFi password generation.
3. **CVE-2026-88956**: Authentication vulnerability in the root account via UART interface.
4. **CVE-2026-81630**: Inadequate verification of firmware updates.

These vulnerabilities pose significant risks, especially in critical infrastructure sectors like Transportation Systems, with deployments worldwide. Botslab has not yet responded to CISA's requests for collaboration to mitigate these issues.

### User Advisory:
Users of affected versions are encouraged to reach out to Botslab for further information and guidance on securing their devices.
