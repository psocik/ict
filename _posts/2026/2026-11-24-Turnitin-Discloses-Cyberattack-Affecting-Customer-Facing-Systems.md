## Turnitin Discloses Cyberattack Affecting Customer-Facing Systems

🚨 **Educational technology company Turnitin** has disclosed that it detected unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing systems and has deployed its incident response plan. Turnitin is a major education technology firm with a global presence, best known for its AI-generated writing detection, digital grading, academic research integrity checks, and examination proctoring. According to its website, its software is used in over 16,000 institutions across 185 countries and serves 71 million students.

In a public statement, **CEO Chris Caren** says that the incident is being investigated with help from external cybersecurity specialists, and law enforcement and cyber regulators have been notified. "We are writing to let you know that we recently identified unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing Turnitin systems," reads the letter from the CEO on the platform's website. "Upon identifying the matter, we implemented our incident response plan, initiated an investigation with external cybersecurity specialists, and notified law enforcement and cyber regulators."

Turnitin assures that its systems are fully operational and safe to use, noting that the affected systems do not collect government identification credentials or institutional financial information. The company stated that its investigation into the incident remains ongoing and has not disclosed any details about the nature and scope of the unauthorized activity. Turnitin continues to monitor its systems for further unauthorized activity, indicating that it is still early to guarantee that the threat has been eliminated completely.

The company has not disclosed whether any user data was accessed or stolen, when the incident occurred, and how many individuals might have been impacted. As of writing, no ransomware groups have publicly taken responsibility for the attack at Turnitin.
