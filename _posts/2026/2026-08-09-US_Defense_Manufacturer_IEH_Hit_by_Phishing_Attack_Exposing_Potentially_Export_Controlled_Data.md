## U.S. Defense Manufacturer IEH Hit by Phishing Attack 🚨

IEH Corporation, a prominent U.S. defense and aerospace manufacturer based in Brooklyn, New York, has fallen victim to a phishing attack that compromised its Microsoft 365 inbox. This breach has potentially exposed sensitive military data and emails.

### What Happened?

On August 4, 2026, IEH Corporation discovered a cybersecurity incident where an unauthorized actor accessed an employee's Microsoft 365 mailbox. The company took immediate action to contain the breach. According to their 8-K filing with the SEC, the attack was executed through a deceptive link that appeared to be a legitimate Microsoft document-sharing link. Unfortunately, an employee entered their credentials on a fraudulent login page, granting the attacker access to the mailbox.

### Impact of the Breach

The attacker accessed various sensitive information, including emails, attachments, customer data, and engineering documents. While no data exfiltration has been confirmed, the implications of exposing export-controlled information are serious, as it could lead to federal violations under ITAR and EAR regulations.

### Conclusion

The phishing attack highlights the vulnerabilities that even small companies in the defense supply chain face. IEH reported nearly $30 million in revenue for fiscal year 2026 and is currently investigating the incident. The company assures that it has no evidence that the breach will materially affect its business operations moving forward.
