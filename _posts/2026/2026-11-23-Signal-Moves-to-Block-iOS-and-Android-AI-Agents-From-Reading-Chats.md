## Signal Moves to Block AI Agents from Reading Chats 🚫

Signal has taken significant steps to prevent AI agents integrated into iOS and Android from capturing users' chats. In a blog post dated November 20, Meredith Whittaker, president of the Signal Foundation, described these AI agents as "an existential threat to Signal." 

### Key Measures Implemented:
- **Screen Security on Android:** Signal will automatically activate Screen Security when it detects an AI assistant attempting to read the screen. 
- **User Control:** Users can disable this protection, but doing so may expose their messages and contacts to AI agents. 
- **iOS 27 Features:** Signal is preventing screenshots and has introduced a setting called "Block Siri AI." 
- **macOS Limitations:** On macOS 27, Signal acknowledged the lack of reliable methods to stop screenshots, advising users to add Signal to the Siri Excluded Apps list. 

In 2025, Signal began blocking screenshots by default on Windows 11 to prevent Microsoft Recall from capturing sensitive information. 

### Concerns About Third-Party Agents:
Signal also highlighted the risks posed by third-party agents like Meta Muse, which utilize accessibility settings to access app data. They have requested Apple, Google, and Microsoft to provide a documented method for apps to opt out of AI access to their screens and files without disrupting functionality. 

Whittaker expressed cautious optimism regarding discussions with major operating system providers, emphasizing the importance of user privacy in the evolving landscape of AI technology.
